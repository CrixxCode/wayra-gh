## Platform

web

## Users

Wayra is primarily for hotel staff who operate and manage the lodging business: reception, administration, operations, managers, and owners. The product also exposes selected public workflows for the hotels' clients, especially searching allied hotels, requesting a reservation, and completing online check-in before arrival.

## Product Purpose

Wayra centralizes hotel management in a single web platform. It supports the operational path from the first reservation through rooms, guests, services, billing, reports, and financial control.

Success means hotel teams can manage daily work with less fragmentation, clearer operational state, and shared information across roles.

## Positioning

Wayra's core difference is not a single isolated module. Its value is connecting the hotel's information and processes end to end in one platform: reservation intake, room operation, guests, check-in and check-out, payments, reports, and financial control.

## Operating Context

Hotel staff use Wayra as a browser-based operational system for daily work. Public users can interact with specific guest-facing surfaces without entering the internal hotel dashboard.

The system is built for small and medium hotels, hostels, aparthotels, and tourist lodgings that need to replace scattered manual tools with one organized workflow.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed capabilities include:

- Public landing page with demo request flow.
- Public allied hotel search and reservation request flow.
- Public online check-in for hotel guests.
- Session-based staff access.
- Multi-hotel SaaS administration.
- Role-based access control.
- Hotel configuration.
- Clients and guests.
- Reservations, rooms, check-in, and check-out.
- Services, packages, and promotions.
- Billing, payments, refunds, and credit notes.
- Finance, expenses, reports, inventory, cleaning, maintenance, and notifications.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is Wayra. The interface language and product copy are Spanish. The product should preserve the distinction between internal hotel staff workflows and public guest/client workflows.

## Product Principles

- Centralize the hotel's work instead of scattering it across disconnected tools.
- Make operational state clear for the staff member who needs to act next.
- Keep guest-facing flows focused and separate from internal administration.
- Preserve trust by avoiding unsupported marketing claims.
- Support role-aware, multi-hotel operation as a core product truth.
